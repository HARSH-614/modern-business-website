About S. Baruah Foodyverse

Rooted in Assam. Inspired by India.

S. Baruah Foodyverse is designed around a singular vision: celebrating the rich, organic culinary roots of Assam while weaving a grand tapestry of iconic regional dishes from across India.

🐟

Authentic Assamese Cuisine

Honoring timeless recipes including Ou Tenga Masor Tenga, Khorisa Gahori, Koldil preparations, and aromatic Joha rice.

🍛

Pan-India Exploration

Curated specialties from Old Delhi charcoal-smoked delicacies to fragrant coastal curries and royal Hyderabadi Dum Biryani.

🌿

Serene Garden Ambience

Peaceful open-air dining under lush greenery at Pabhoi Panchali, crafted for family meals, couples, and student groups.
